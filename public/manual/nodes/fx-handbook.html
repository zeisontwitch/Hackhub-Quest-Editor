Open handbook

Effects  fx.handbook

What it does

Opens a page of the game's own handbook for the player.

When you'd use it

Use it when the player needs background the story should not have to spell out: what a company does, who owns a domain, how a protocol behaves.

Pick a page that already exists in the game, or type the identifier of one your mod adds.

In the game, this looks like

The handbook opens on the page you chose.

Fields

Article

The in-game article the player lands on. Pick a known page, or type any article id.
Watch out
An identifier that matches no article opens nothing, and the player gets no explanation of why.

Category

The handbook section the article sits under.

Wires

SocketDirectionWhat to connect
Ininput Whatever should run immediately before this node.
Outoutput Whatever should happen after this node runs.

Then runs next When tests a condition Unlocks opens an objective Data carries a value

A wire only joins two sockets of the same colour, and the editor draws the wire in that colour. That is what the colours are for: match the colours and the connection takes, mismatch them and it will not.

Common mistakes

  • The article belongs to another mod. The player does not have it, so the handbook opens on nothing. Point at an article your own mod ships.

Messages you might see

  • Unreachable — nothing leads to this node, so the article never opens

Related nodes

  • Pay the player — Deposits money in the player's bank.
  • Charge the player — Takes money out of the player's account.
  • Notify — Puts a message on the player's screen.
  • Ask player — Asks the player a one-line question.
  • Set quest data — Stores a value for later.
  • Claim another quest — Starts the next quest.
  • Complete quest — Finishes the current quest.
  • Retire quest — Removes the current quest without completion.
  • Unclaim quest — Removes a claimed quest.
  • Run terminal command — Runs a command in the player's terminal.
