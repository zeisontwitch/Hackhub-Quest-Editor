Quest start

Quest lifecycle  entry.start

What it does

Runs the moment the quest is claimed. Anything the story needs in place before the player acts goes here.

When you'd use it

This is the front door of your quest. Wire the opening to its Out socket: the briefing mail, the first objective, whatever the player should meet first.

You can have more than one, and the game runs all of them when the quest is claimed. Splitting a long opening across two is fine.

It has no input socket, because nothing runs before it.

In the game, this looks like

The player accepts the quest and this fires. Whatever you wired after it happens at once, before they have done anything.

Fields

This node has nothing to configure. It marks a moment in the story and runs whatever is wired after it.

Wires

SocketDirectionWhat to connect
Outoutput Whatever should happen after this node runs.

Then runs next When tests a condition Unlocks opens an objective Data carries a value

A wire only joins two sockets of the same colour, and the editor draws the wire in that colour. That is what the colours are for: match the colours and the connection takes, mismatch them and it will not.

Step by step: add this node to a quest

  1. Find Quest start under Quest lifecycle in the palette on the left.
  2. Drag it onto the canvas, or open the one your quest already has.
  3. Drag a wire from its Out socket into the first node of your story.
  4. Look at the status bar. An Empty warning here means nothing is wired to Out.

Common mistakes

  • Something that has to survive a reload is wired here. This runs once, when the quest is claimed. After the player reloads, anything you armed here is gone. Put that in On start & reload instead.
  • Nothing is wired to Out. The quest starts and then stops. The player gets an empty journal and no idea what to do next.

Messages you might see

  • Empty — nothing is wired to Out, so the node does nothing

Related nodes

  • On start & reload — Runs on claim, and again on every load.
  • On quest complete — Runs after the quest is completed.
  • On quest abandoned — Runs if the player gives up.
