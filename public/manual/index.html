Three things worth knowing before you start

  • A quest never completes on its own. That sounds like a bug and is worth understanding properly — why quests do not complete.
  • Your editor settings are not part of the mod. Theme, font, grid and wire feel stay on your machine. See Settings.
  • Exporting is not saving. The .zip is the finished mod, not something you can open and edit again. See Export is not a save.
