Node reference

All 39 node types you can add, grouped the way the palette groups them.

Every page here follows the same shape: what the node does, when you would use it, what the player experiences, every setting it has, what to wire into it, how to add it, and the ways it usually goes wrong.

Quest lifecycle

NodeWhat it does
Quest startentry.startRuns once when the quest begins. Setup goes here.
On start & reloadentry.loadRuns on claim and every load. Listeners go here.
On quest completeentry.completeRuns after a Complete quest node, auto-complete, or Complete button finishes the quest.
On quest abandonedentry.abandonRuns if the player gives up. Cleanup goes here.
